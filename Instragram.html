Pro Tips for Designing a High-Converting Instagram Bio

To keep your profile looking polished and accessible to all followers, keep these simple best practices in mind:

  • Mix Fancy Fonts with Standard Text: Use stylish fonts for your display name, section headers, or key highlights, while keeping important contact details or website links in clean, easy-to-read text.
  • Mind the 150-Character Bio Limit: Instagram caps bios at 150 characters. Some ornate Unicode styles take up extra space, so preview your layout after saving to ensure nothing gets cut off.
  • Keep Your Handle (@username) Simple: Instagram only allows standard letters, numbers, periods, and underscores in your actual @handle, so save your fancy typography for your bold Name field and bio lines.
