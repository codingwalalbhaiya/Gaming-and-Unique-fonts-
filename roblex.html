Important Rules for Roblox Display Names

Roblox applies strict safety and character filters to keep the platform friendly for all ages. Keep these quick guidelines in mind when choosing your font:

  • Stay Between 3 and 20 Characters: Roblox Display Names must be at least 3 characters long and no more than 20 characters. Choose compact Unicode styles so your name fits comfortably inside the limit.
  • Use Clean Latin-Based Unicode Styles: While Roblox supports many stylish Latin variations, small caps, and subtle symbols, overly heavy decorative brackets or non-standard symbols may trigger an "Invalid Display Name" message.
